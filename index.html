About Us

Talent exists everywhere, but opportunity does not.

Lilo Foundation was created from a simple but deeply personal belief: talent exists everywhere, but opportunity does not.

Growing up in rural KwaZulu-Natal, founder Nonjabulo Matomela experienced firsthand how exposure, encouragement, and support can completely change the direction of a young person’s life. Opportunities such as bursaries and educational support only became possible because teachers recognised potential in her early on and encouraged her ambitions.

Today, Nonjabulo is an environmental scientist. However, experiences within her own family and community continue to highlight how limited career awareness can still be for many young people growing up in underserved areas.

Finance Director Nkululeko Ngwenya shares a similar experience. Through poetry, creative arts programmes, school competitions, and academic opportunities usually reserved for high-performing learners, he was introduced to worlds and possibilities he had never encountered growing up.

Those experiences helped him discover a deep love for poetry, storytelling, and self-expression, while also showing him that young people from communities like his could belong in spaces they had previously only admired from a distance.

Together, these experiences shaped the vision behind Lilo Foundation.

Rural and township schools do not only face financial barriers. Many learners also face limited digital access, lack of exposure to careers and universities, limited mentorship opportunities, and little awareness of what opportunities even exist.

Lilo Foundation exists to help close that gap by creating access to awareness, opportunity, mentorship, career guidance, and educational support for young people who deserve the chance to imagine bigger futures for themselves.

Our Programmes

Practical initiatives designed to improve access and opportunity.

Career Access Expos

Community-focused career awareness events that expose learners to universities, professionals, scholarships, and career pathways they may not otherwise encounter.

Mobile Application Support

Providing learners with access to WiFi, laptops, printing support, university applications, bursary guidance, and NSFAS assistance.

Lilo Scholars Network

Connecting high-potential learners to mentorship, opportunities, scholarships, and supportive networks.
